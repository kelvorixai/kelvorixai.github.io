Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This page covers the Kelvorix AI hub (kelvorixai.github.io) itself. Each individual site (RotaWage, HomeStack, WageWorks, VisaTrail, MoveMeter, TrueCostAuto, CompoundClear, ChainClear) has its own privacy policy for its own pages.

This hub page has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no newsletter box. It's a directory linking out to the individual Kelvorix AI sites — nothing you type is collected here because there's nothing to type.

This is standard for ad-supported publishing and is covered by Google's own policies, not a separate tracking system we've built.

You can see and adjust how Google personalizes ads for you at adssettings.google.com, and read more about how Google's advertising partners use data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Who this hub is for

This page is general-audience content and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect any information from children, which is easy to be confident about, since we don't collect personal information from anyone here.

Changes to this policy
