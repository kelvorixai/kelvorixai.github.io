About Kelvorix AI

A small family of independent explainer sites, each focused on one topic in real depth.

Kelvorix AI publishes plain-English, data-driven guides across a handful of separate sites — UAE money and living costs, car ownership, general personal-finance mechanics, and crypto. Each site sticks to one subject rather than trying to cover everything, and every guide is written to explain how something actually works, not to sell a product or push a recommendation.

Nothing published across these sites is personalized financial, tax, legal, or immigration advice, and none of it is affiliated with any bank, fund manager, exchange, developer, or platform mentioned. For decisions specific to your own situation, speak with a licensed professional in the relevant field.

This project is run pseudonymously. Each site under Kelvorix AI has its own privacy policy and terms of use — this page and the domain-wide privacy policy cover the hub itself.
